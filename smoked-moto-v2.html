About

Smoked Moto started because most plate mounts are built for something loud. Combustion graphics, chrome, flame decals — none of it fits a bike that doesn't make a sound.

We make one thing: plates, built specifically for e-bikes and e-motos. Lighter mounts, cleaner lines, finishes that hold up to rain and road salt instead of engine heat.

If it doesn't look right on an electric build, we don't make it.
